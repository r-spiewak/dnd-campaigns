## Background 

Party was recruited among many others to stop a blight that has taken the land by storm. Plant zombies have been taking over other living creatures on the continent. They are only active by day, and at night they are completely motionless. Come sunrise, they become extremely aggressive again. No one knows how they transmit the virus, but it looks like if they get ahold of someone then vines come out of their mouths and start infecting the target, and Lesser Restoration (or Greater Restoration) or a surgical procedure in which the party has been prepped can stop the infection from turning into a zombie.

## Party:
- Ruffus is a tiefling sword bard, highly charismatic, but has some sword skills too.
- Tony, a warforged artificer.

## Game Play

### Initial Battle

The party has investigated and found a temple at the top of a mountain, and they see a party of four zombie plants (one is a hill giant). Ruffus greets them, they turn and attack.

Ruffus goes to the yellow plant zombie and attacks with his Rapier, 9 damage. Again, 13 more damage. Bonus action Bardic Inspiration to Tony.
Green zombie runs to Tony. But it misses.
Pink zombie runs up to Ruffus and attacks. 24 damage. And failed con save, so total max hp reduced to the current amount.
Yellow zombie misses Ruffus.
Tony shoots Lightning at green zombie, 2 blasts for 23 damage. Not bloodied, but close.
Hill zombie blue moves up to Tony and swings and misses. Second swing misses so badly that it hits the green zombie for 14 damage and forcing him prone.

Ruffus returns the favor to the yellow guy. One Rapier hits for 13 damage. But second swing misses, and yellow tries to grab Ruffus but Ruffus escapes nimbly.
Green guy gets up swings at Tony and misses so badly that he hits the hill giant instead for 24 damage. Hill giant fails his con save, so that's now his max hp. Blighted limb lands on hill giant and rips away flesh, revealing more bark underneath.
Pink zombie swings at Ruffus, for 13 damage. Ruffus makes the con save (so his max is still 43, but not 67).
Yellow zombie swings and misses so badly that he hits the pink zombiw instead for 26 damage. Pink zombie succeeds on the con save though.
Tony crits the green zombie, dealing 13 damage, killing him. Second shot at the yellow zombie, 10 damage, disintigrating it with lightning.
Hill giant swings at Tony and hits one for 15 damage, and the second swing misses so bad and brings him to the pink zombie and hits him for 16 damage. And hill giant fell over (so he's Prone).

Ruffus swings his rapier and hits the pink zombie for 7 damage. Second swing rapier for 9 damage, splitting him in half and killing him. 
Tony shoots twice at the hill giant zombie, both hit for a total of 14 damage.
Hill giant gets up and crits against Tony, dealing 40 damage. Second swing, for another 17 damage (Tony is doen to 10 hp).

Ruffus casts Ray of Sickness, but misses.
Tony takes two shots with his lightning, both hit for a total of 22 damage. Hill giant is starting to look bad.
Hill gant swings at Tony, misses the first and dealing 19 damage on the second, knocking Tony unconscious.

Ruffus taunts hill giant with Poison Spray but misses, and also casts Healing Word at Tony healing for 10 damage.
Tony casts Invisibility to move away from the hill giant 35 feet down the mountain towards the temple.
Hill giant looks around confused, because both enemies just disappeared.

### Down the Mountain

Ruffus and Tony move farther down the mountain to the temple, and go in. Through ancient texts the party had discovered that the plague is centered around this temple.
There are etchings on the front wall of the temple:
"Bring forth light, and thorns shall wake,
A garden stirred for folly’s sake.
So heed this truth within the gloom:
The written word will seal your doom.
Let color guide where names deceive,
And deathless roots shall grant reprieve."

The temple is completely dark. Ruffus sees an unnerving amount of plant zombies in the temple, and Tony casts Darkvision and also sees them. Tony is also still Invisible.
Ruffus sees a large statue on the other side of the room, that has four sockets in the bottom of the statue, and four stones sitting in front of the sockets.
Tony and Ruffus carefully walk through and around the zombies. On the top of the sockets there are etchings. 
The first one says Ruby.
The second says Sapphire.
The thirs says Emerald.
The fourth says Topaz.

Ruffus casts Tiny Hut around the party and the statue and stones, makes it dimly lit, but opaque from the outside. Dim light plus Darkvision makes it light enough to see.
The Ruby stone says Topaz, the Sapphire stone says Emerald, the Emerald stone says Ruby, and the Topaz stone says Sapphire.
Party puts the stones in the named slots based on the color of the stones.
As each stone goes into the slot, it starts glowing. As the four stones are placed, the statue starts to glow, and finally a pulse of light spreads out from the statue. As it touches each zombie, roots spread out into the ground, turning it into a tree.

### Conclusion

Plague over. World saved.
