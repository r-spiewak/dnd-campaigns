## Game Play

### Deeper Into the Cave

In the next room along the path, there are more Kua-toa. 

Ack and Joe go back the other way, and back to the other path from the entrance. 
But there is another alcove of Kua-toa in that other path, and Fireballing that wouldn't leave them a path to go. 

Ack pulls Joe's shirt to get his attention and makes some wierd gestures, but Joe doesn't understand. 
So Joe Messages Ack. 
Ack responds that he can Darkness the two of them to get past the Kua-toa, or run them past. 
So Ack and Joe decide to stealthily sneak past them, but suddenly the sound of eating stops as they go past. 

Ack starts to hear the call of "Someone's here, let's ask them" in Undercommon (which Ack understands). 
Joe Messages Ack asking "What's going on?"
Ack says, "They heard us". 
Joe suggest Messaging them asking what they want to ask.
Ack says "sure". 
So Joe does, and from the conversation Joe and Ack decide that the Kua-toa had been eating fish, but then evil creatures (frog like bodies with pirhanna heads, who ride sharks, called Sahuagin) came. The Kua-toa say those things ate all the fish, and ask if Ack and Joe can help.
Then the Kua-toa show Ack and Joe the other end of the cave, where some creatures run away out through the end of the cave. 
The Kua-toa shout "They were watching us!" and they also call for about 15 of their party who aren't there. 

### Out back

So Ack and Joe follow the things out from the other end of the cave. 

They walk out of the cave and hear the Baby Shark song start playing. 
They see a shark family, and a dragon turtle talking to the baby shark. But no fish. 

Then Ack jumps into the water... and scrambles back up the beach wall as a blue shark swims at him with great speed. He runs up the wall and avoids the shark.

They decide to Message the creatures running from them: 
>>> (Party) What's happening? 
<<< (sahuagin) Get out, don't mess with our good thing! 
>>> What is it, and can we help? 
<<< We found food, and we'll be leaving soon. 
>>> What's going on with the little shark and the dragon turtle? 

All the sharks go towards the conversation. The Sahuagin ride the sharks to the conversation. Ack and Joe see the sharks attack the baby shark, because it's not supposed to be talking to the dragon turtle. The other sharks put it on a baby leash and drag it further underwater away from the dragon turtle.

Ack and Joe Message the dragon turtle, asking if it knows what's going on.
<<< I cannot break client confidentiality.
>>> What's your profession?
<<< Child trauma therapist
>>> Can you help us with the shark problem?
<<< You must first break me out of the sediment that has enclosed around me.
>>> Sure!

Ack goes to swim over to take a look. 
Joe asks if it would rather be bigger or smaller (temporarily), it chooses smaller so the island doesn't tilt. 
Joe casts Enlarge/Reduce on it to make it smaller. 
The dragon turtle is free, un-Reduced, and dives into the water. 

There is a lot of blood, and it resurfaces with the yellow baby shark (bruised and battered, but not worse for the wear). 

### Conclusion

Fish come back, Kua-toa come back. Ack and Joe saved the ecosystem!

But Baby shark isn't happy because he's hurt and his therapist just killed its family. But Ack and Joe don't have any poitions or healing capabilities or anything anyway...
