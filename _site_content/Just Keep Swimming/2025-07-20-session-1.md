## Background

Aboard a ship, in a storm. A lot of rain pounding down on deck, visibility is low. Turbulent waters, thrashing. Each member of the party grabbed something while ship splintered and sank. 

The party wakes up on the beach of previously uncharted island. As they start to wake up, they hear screaming and dragging. Ack hears the sound of eating also, ravenous and feverish, like pirhannas. 

## Party:
- Ack is a level 10 grung shadowmonk, and did fine in the crash as he's aquatic. Ack doesn't speak Common.
- Joe is a level 8 Tiefling Acolyte Evocation Wizard. He grabbed his packed pack (since he didn't unpack), and grabbed a plank of wood. 

## Game Play

### Loot the Corpses

Joe looks around and sees drag lines of other bodies getting dragged off into the distance. Also corpses. 
Joe loots the corpses. Joe finds a Cube of Force, a Horn of Valhalla (brass), Ring of Psychic Resistance, Ring of Animal Influence, Ring of Protection, A +2 Wand of the War Mage, an Instrument of the Bards Cli lyre, and a Necklace of Prayer Beads. And also 25 gp. And a Mace of Smiting. Ring of protection requires attunement (and gives +1 AC), as does ring of psychic resistance, and the wand of the war mage (which means spellcasting mod is +2 what it was before).
Ack loots also. He gets an Amulet of Health, a Figurine of Wonderous Power (bronze griffin), Ring of Acid resistance, Ring of the Ram (battering), +1 Armor, Peeping Tom Glasses (Ring of X-Ray vision), Figurine of Wonderous Power (ebony lye), and a Staff of Charming. And 25 gp. And a mace of Terror. Attuned to ring of health, ring of xray vision, and mace of terror.

[GM limit of 8 short rests per day.]

Ack invokes Musician feat. Encouraging Song, Joe gains Heroic Inspiration (can re-roll a die). 
The party all started with 5000gp because they're such high level adventurers. Ack started with 7000 gp.


### Into the Cave

Ack and Joe start following the drag marks. They go into a cave, and they see lots of blood and drag marks. 
The tunnel splits, and Ack and Joe choose one of the paths.
They go along a path, a damp cave tunnel system, everything is wet. They are following drag marks. They round a few bends in this dark cave system. 

There are a lot of tracks and noise coming from the path up ahead. They come across a scene of these humanoid shape pirhanna fish monsters that are just eating the flesh off the bones of corpses from people from the ship. Joe knows that these are Kuo-toa. 
Ack starts chriping danger.
Joe Messages him "What?" 
And Ack responds "Danger, we should leave." 
Joe asks if he should cast fireball in there, and Ack responds sure, and Ack and Joe back up and Joe casts Fireball and roasts them. 

And now the room is basically lava. Joe has half blind, and Ack is blinded, for not covering their eyes enough before casting Fireball.
